What's New in v1.1.5:

• Recurring Reminders Overhaul: Flawless timezone-aware scheduling, strict mathematical next-occurrence calculations to prevent skipped days, and a new split view separating "One-Off" and "Repeating" tasks.

• Advanced Memories Filtering: A completely revamped memories screen featuring cascading multi-select filters (Audio, Text, Starred, Transactions, Reminders) and dynamic time ranges (Recent vs All Time) with live counts.

• Rock-Solid Data Management: Hardened and more reliable deletion logic across the app, ensuring your data is handled safely.

• Responsive UI Improvements: Core app screens have been updated to be fully responsive and adapt gracefully to different screen sizes.

What's New in v1.1.6:

• Interactive Home Screen Widgets: Add notes instantly, view upcoming reminders, check personal finances, and review Splitwise balances directly from your Android home screen with beautiful, dynamically-sized widgets.

• Perfected Splitwise Balances: Your personal net balances in the Splitwise tab now accurately net out borrowed and repaid amounts to give you a clean "Owed to You" and "You Owe" summary. Settled debts naturally disappear from the list.

• Smart Background Sync: Your home screen widgets stay seamlessly up to date in the background and intelligently launch straight to the correct app tab when tapped.
